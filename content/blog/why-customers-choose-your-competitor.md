It is one of the most common things business owners tell us: *"We are better, but they get the customers."*

Customers searching online cannot see how good your work is. They can only see what Google shows them. In the few seconds they spend deciding, a handful of signals do the choosing.

## 1. They appear first, and you do not appear at all

Most people call one of the first few businesses they see. If you are not in that small group, quality never gets a chance to matter.

**Fix it:** Create or claim your Google Business Profile, choose the most accurate primary category and complete every section. A complete profile is the entry ticket.

## 2. They have more reviews, and newer ones

A business with many recent reviews looks active and trusted. A business with three reviews from two years ago looks quiet, even if it is not.

**Fix it:** Make asking for reviews a habit. Send your review link after every finished job, while the customer is still happy. Reply to every review so people see a real person behind the business.

## 3. Their profile has real photos

Photos answer questions before a customer asks them. What does the place look like? Is it clean? Does the work look good? An empty profile leaves people guessing, and guessing feels risky.

**Fix it:** Add photos of your premises, your team and finished work. Add a few new ones every month.

## 4. They are easier to contact

If a customer has to search for your number, find your hours or work out where you are, many will simply tap the next result.

**Fix it:** Make sure your phone number, opening hours and Maps pin are correct. On your website, put a tap-to-call button and a WhatsApp button near the top of every page.

## 5. Their website looks trustworthy, or you have none

Many customers check the website before calling. A slow, outdated or missing site makes a business feel less established.

**Fix it:** You do not need a big website. A fast, mobile-friendly site with your services, photos, location and clear contact buttons does the job.

## Where to start

You do not need to fix everything at once. Most businesses get the biggest improvement from this order:

1. A complete, verified Google profile
2. A steady flow of real reviews
3. Fresh photos every month
4. Clear contact buttons everywhere
5. A simple, fast website
