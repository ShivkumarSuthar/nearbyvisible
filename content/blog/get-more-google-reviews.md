Reviews are often the deciding factor when someone compares businesses on Google. The good news: most happy customers are willing to leave one. They just need to be asked at the right moment, in the easiest possible way.

## Get your review link

Your Google Business Profile has a "Ask for reviews" or "Share review form" option. It gives you a short link that opens the review box directly. Save it somewhere you can copy it quickly, like a pinned note on your phone.

## Ask at the right moment

Ask every customer, not only the ones you expect to be happy. Google does not allow picking and choosing who gets asked. What you can choose is the timing:

- Right after a job is finished.
- With the final bill or delivery message.
- At the end of a visit, while the experience is fresh.

A short, personal message works best:

> "Thank you for choosing us! If you have a minute, an honest Google review really helps. Here is the link: …"

## Make it a habit, not a campaign

A burst of reviews followed by silence looks odd. A steady few every week looks natural and keeps your profile fresh. Pick one simple routine and stick to it, for example: send the review link with every final bill or delivery message.

## Make it easy

- **Use a QR code** at your counter or on your visiting card that opens the review link.
- **Keep the message short.** One line and the link.
- **Do not ask for a specific star rating.** Just ask for honest feedback.

## Reply to every review

Replying shows customers there is a real person behind the business.

- **Good review:** thank them by name and mention what they liked.
- **Bad review:** stay calm, apologise for their experience, and offer to fix it offline. Future customers read your reply more than the complaint.

## What not to do

These can get reviews removed or your profile penalised:

- Buying reviews or using review services.
- Writing reviews for yourself, or asking staff and family to do it.
- Offering discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews.
- Asking only happy customers while filtering out others through a "rate us first" gate.

## A simple weekly routine

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| After every job | Send the review link |
| Every week | Reply to all new reviews |
| Every month | Check your rating and review count |
